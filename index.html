Compensation

Bonuses

How we recognize

Going the Extra Mile

Discretionary bonuses are extra payments on top of regular salary or fee. They are not automatic or guaranteed. The Company may choose to award one when someone has done something genuinely worth recognizing.

Earn monthly, get paid annually

Bonus credit can be earned any month of the year. Everything is collected and paid once per year, in October or December, as a year-end thank-you for contributions made along the way.

01

The big picture

Bonuses are open to everyone at The Banished: full-time employees, part-time employees, remote contractors, and freelancers. If you contribute, you are eligible.

02

What can earn a bonus

  • Going beyond scope: work clearly outside the original agreement or role, delivered well.
  • Driving a company win: actions, decisions, or creative output that led to measurable revenue, growth, or savings.
  • Doing something remarkable: work that stood out, even if it does not fit a neat category.
03

The approval process

  1. Your direct manager spots the contribution and prepares a recommendation.
  2. The recommendation is reviewed through the appropriate leadership chain.
  3. Finance confirms approval and schedules payment for the next available window.

You do not need to nominate yourself. If something is worth recognizing, your manager will know.

04

Payment windows

October

If everything is approved and ready, the bonus arrives before the end of October.

December

If October is not feasible, Finance will confirm and payment moves to December.

You will hear from us at least 10 business days before the payment date.

05

Taxes and practical notes

  • W-2 employee bonuses go through payroll and taxes are handled automatically.
  • Contractors and freelancers receive the full amount and handle their own taxes.
  • A bonus one year does not guarantee one the next. Each review is independent.
  • The amount is always at the Company's discretion, with no set minimum or maximum.
Questions? Contact HR at hr@the-banished.com.

We are a team spread across borders, time zones, and cultures. This guide lays out every day off we observe as a company, plus the flexibility we offer for those who call a different country home.

A note on who gets what

Paid holiday means employees receive the day off with full pay. Non-working day means contractors and freelancers are not expected to work, but the day is unpaid unless the contract says otherwise.
